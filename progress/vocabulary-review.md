# Vocabulary Review (Spaced Repetition)

Vocabulary is reviewed using a **Leitner box system**. Each item moves up a box when recalled correctly (German → English *and* English → German, **with article and plural for nouns**) and drops back to Box 1 when missed.

| Box | Review frequency | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Every lesson | New or recently missed |
| **2** | Every 2nd lesson | Recalled once |
| **3** | Every 4th lesson | Recalled twice |
| **4** | Every 8th lesson | Recalled three times |
| **5** | Cumulative tests only | Long-term memory |

A noun counts as "recalled" **only if the article is correct**. *Tag* without *der* is a miss.

**Recommended:** also keep these words in **Anki** (free spaced-repetition app). Make cards in both directions, and for nouns put the article and plural on the card: `der Tag, -e`.
