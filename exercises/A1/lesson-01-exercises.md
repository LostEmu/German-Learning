### Exercise 1: *du* or *Sie*? Which greeting? *(register)*
For each situation: (i) would you use **du** or **Sie**? (ii) Write an appropriate **greeting or farewell** in German.

1. 8:00 am. You meet your new landlord, Herr Brunner, for the first time.
2. 2:00 pm. You walk into a bakery in Zurich.
3. A fellow ETH student, about 22, sits down next to you before the first lecture.
4. 7:30 pm. You arrive at a dinner hosted by Professor Anna Keller.
5. 11:30 pm. Your housemate says they are going to bed.
6. You are leaving an appointment with an official at the migration office.

### Exercise 2: Verb forms *(conjugation)*
Fill in the correct form of the verb in brackets.

1. Wie ______ du? *(heißen)*
2. Ich ______ aus Australien. *(kommen)*
3. Woher ______ Sie? *(kommen)*
4. Wo ______ du? *(wohnen)*
5. Ich ______ Elektrotechnik und Physik. *(studieren)*
6. Entschuldigung, ich ______ nicht. *(verstehen)*
7. Du ______ Student, und ich ______ auch Student. *(sein)*

### Exercise 3: Question words *(wie, wo, woher)*
Fill in **wie**, **wo** or **woher**.

1. ______ kommst du? – Aus Deutschland.
2. ______ wohnen Sie? – In Zürich.
3. ______ heißt du? – Lena.
4. ______ geht es Ihnen? – Gut, danke.

### Exercise 4: Error correction *(analysis)*
Each sentence contains **exactly one** mistake. Rewrite the sentence correctly **and explain** what was wrong.

1. Was heißt du?
2. Wo kommen Sie? *(asking about someone's origin)*
3. Woher kommst Sie?
4. Ich bin ein Student.
5. Guten Abend, Frau Keller! Wie geht es dir?
6. Ich wohne in Zürich und Ich studiere Physik.

### Exercise 5: Translation into German *(production)*
1. Good evening, Mrs Schmidt! How are you?
2. Hi! I'm *[your name]*. What's your name? *(to a fellow student)*
3. Where are you from? – I'm from Australia, and I live in Perth. *(formal)*
4. I'm a student. I study electrical engineering and physics.
5. Pardon? I don't understand. Once more, please.
6. Thank you very much! – You're welcome. Goodbye! *(formal)*
