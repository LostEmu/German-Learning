# Adjectives & Adverbs

Adjectives learned, with comparative and superlative forms added once comparison is taught (A2).
Adjective endings (*guten Morgen*, *gute Nacht*) are taught at A2. Until then, endings appear only in fixed chunks.

**Running total:** 3
