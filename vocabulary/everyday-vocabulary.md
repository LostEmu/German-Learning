# Everyday Vocabulary

Master list of everyday vocabulary, grouped by lesson. **Nouns** always show article, gender and plural. **Verbs** are in [verbs.md](verbs.md), **adjectives** in [adjectives.md](adjectives.md), **academic/subject words** in [academic-german.md](academic-german.md).

**Gender key:** m = masculine (*der*), f = feminine (*die*), n = neuter (*das*); — = no plural / not applicable

**Running total:** 61 items (Lesson 1). *Updated after every lesson.*
