## Personal: talking about your studies (Lesson 1)

| Noun | Gender | Plural | English | Pronunciation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| die Physik | f | — | physics | [fyˈziːk]: ph = f, y = ü, stress on the last syllable |
| die Elektrotechnik | f | — | electrical engineering | [eˈlɛktroˌtɛçnɪk]: ich-sound |
| die Mathematik | f | — | mathematics | [matemaˈtiːk]: th = t |

**Usage:** *Ich studiere Elektrotechnik und Physik.* (no article after *studieren*)
