## Prepositions met as chunks (Lesson 1)

| Preposition | Meaning | Example | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **aus** | from (origin) | *Ich komme **aus** Australien.* | always followed by the dative (Lesson 24) |
| **in** | in (location) | *Ich wohne **in** Zürich.* | with cities and most countries: no article needed |
| **bis** | until | *Bis bald! Bis morgen!* | |
