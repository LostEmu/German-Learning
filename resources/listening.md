# Listening Resources

Listening and pronunciation can't be fully practised in a text-based lesson. These resources fill that gap. The level shown is the **earliest** point at which each becomes useful.

## Pronunciation (use from Lesson 0)

| Resource | What it is | How to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Forvo** (forvo.com) | Native-speaker recordings of individual words | Look up every new word; imitate; record yourself and compare |
| **Wiktionary** (de.wiktionary.org) | IPA transcription + audio for many words | Check IPA against the rules in Lesson 0 |
| **DWDS** (dwds.de) and **Duden** (duden.de) | Major German dictionaries, often with audio | Authoritative pronunciation and stress |

## Structured courses (A1 →)

| Resource | Level | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **DW: Nicos Weg** (learngerman.dw.com) | A1–B1 | Free video course by Deutsche Welle. Strongly recommended alongside these lessons |
| **Coffee Break German** (podcast) | A1–A2 | Explanations in English, gradually more German |
| **Easy German** (YouTube) | A2 → | Street interviews with German + English subtitles; natural speech |
| **Slow German** (podcast, Annik Rubens) | A2–B1 | Slowly spoken monologues on everyday & cultural topics |

## News & general listening (B1 →)

| Resource | Level | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **DW: Langsam gesprochene Nachrichten** | B1–B2 | Daily news, read slowly, with transcript |
| **logo!** (ZDF) | B1 | Children's news: clear and visual |
| **Die Sendung mit der Maus** (WDR) | B1 | How things work: excellent for technical explanation vocabulary |
| **Tagesschau in 100 Sekunden** | B2 | Short German news bulletin |

## Swiss & academic listening (B2 →)

| Resource | Level | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **SRF** (srf.ch) | B2 → | Swiss public broadcaster. News in Swiss Standard German; many programmes in Swiss German (for later dialect comprehension) |
| **ETH Video Portal** (video.ethz.ch) | B2–C1 | Recorded ETH lectures; Bachelor-level courses are largely taught in German. Some recordings require ETH login |

## Speaking practice with humans

The tutor can't hear your pronunciation. Get **periodic human feedback**:
- A conversation tutor (e.g. via italki), even every few weeks, especially for pronunciation checks
- Language exchange apps (e.g. Tandem) from about A2
- University language centres / German clubs
