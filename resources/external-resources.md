# External Resources

## Dictionaries & reference

| Resource | Use |
|---|---|
| **DWDS** (dwds.de) | Authoritative German–German dictionary with corpus examples, etymology, pronunciation |
| **Duden** (duden.de) | Official spelling authority; gender, plural, pronunciation |
| **LEO** (dict.leo.org) / **dict.cc** | German–English dictionaries with forums for tricky usage |
| **Wiktionary** (de.wiktionary.org) | Full conjugation and declension tables, IPA |
| **Verbformen** (verbformen.de) | Conjugation tables for any verb |

⚠️ **Do not use machine translation (DeepL, Google Translate) for exercises.** It hides your real level and prevents learning. It's fine for checking the meaning of a text you're reading.

## Grammar books

| Book | Level | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Hammer's German Grammar and Usage** (Martin Durrell) | B1–C1 | The standard comprehensive reference grammar in English. Excellent for rigorous explanations |
| **Grimm Grammar** (University of Texas at Austin, free online) | A1–B1 | Clear, free beginner grammar reference |

## Spaced-repetition & apps

| Tool | Use |
|---|---|
| **Anki** (apps.ankiweb.net) | **Recommended.** Flashcards with spaced repetition. Nouns as `der Tag, -e`; both directions |
| Duolingo and similar | At most a light supplement; not enough on its own for real proficiency |

## Official exams

| Exam | Levels | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Goethe-Zertifikat** (Goethe-Institut) | A1–C2 | A1 is called *Start Deutsch 1*. Widely recognised |
| **telc Deutsch** | A1–C1 | Includes *telc Deutsch C1 Hochschule* for university admission |
| **ÖSD** (Austrian) | A1–C2 | Accepted in Switzerland too |
| **TestDaF** | B2–C1 | University-oriented |
| **DSH** | B2–C1 | Set by individual German universities |

## Switzerland-specific

| Resource | Notes |
|---|---|
| **fide** | Switzerland's national framework for language skills for integration (residence permits, naturalisation). Required levels depend on permit type and canton. **Check current official requirements when relevant** |
| **Language Center of the University of Zurich and ETH Zurich** (*Sprachenzentrum der UZH und ETH Zürich*) | Offers German and Swiss German courses to students and staff |
