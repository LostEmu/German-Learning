# Curriculum — B2 (Upper Intermediate)

**CEFR descriptor (B2):** Can understand the main ideas of complex text on both concrete and abstract topics, including technical discussions in their field of specialisation. Can interact with a degree of fluency and spontaneity that makes regular interaction with native speakers quite possible without strain for either party. Can produce clear, detailed text on a wide range of subjects and explain a viewpoint on a topical issue giving the advantages and disadvantages of various options.

**Status:** Not started. *Provisional. This is the major practical-proficiency target.*

---

## Grammar strand

| Topic | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Konjunktiv I & reported speech | News, academic reporting of others' claims (*Der Autor behauptet, die Methode sei …*) |
| Passive alternatives | *sein + zu*, *sich lassen*, *-bar/-lich* adjectives, state passive (*Zustandspassiv*). Very common in technical writing |
| **Extended participial attributes** | *die von uns entwickelte Methode* ("the method developed by us"). **Essential for reading scientific German** |
| Nominalisation & nominal style | Academic register (*die Berechnung der Eigenwerte* vs *man berechnet die Eigenwerte*) |
| Advanced connectors & cohesion | *infolgedessen, demzufolge, hingegen, wohingegen, sofern, indem* |
| Subjective modal verbs | *Er soll …* (reportedly), *Das dürfte …* (probably), *Sie muss …* (must have) |
| Modal particles | doch, ja, mal, eben, halt, wohl: what makes spoken German sound natural |
| Advanced word order | The middle field (*Mittelfeld*), pronoun placement, emphasis |
| Futur II | Assumptions about the past (*Er wird es vergessen haben*) |
| Functional verb constructions (intro) | *zur Verfügung stellen, in Frage kommen, eine Entscheidung treffen* |

## Skills strand

- **Writing:** formal emails (*Sehr geehrte …*), reports, argumentative essays (*Erörterung*), describing graphs and data (*Grafikbeschreibung*)
- **Listening:** university lectures, longer discussions, podcasts at natural speed
- **Speaking:** explaining technical concepts, presenting a short talk, discussing pros and cons
- **Reading:** popular-science magazines, technical documentation, introductory textbooks

## Swiss strand

- Listening to Zurich German in everyday contexts (shops, transport, small talk)
- Key structural differences between Swiss German and Standard German (no simple past; *-li* diminutives; vocabulary and pronunciation differences)
- Workplace and university register in Zurich: who speaks what, when

## B2 exit criteria

1. Pass a B2 checkpoint modelled on Goethe-Zertifikat B2 / telc B2 (≥ 80%, no section below 70%).
2. Write a 250-word argumentative text in formal register.
3. Explain a technical concept from your field in German, understandably and with correct terminology.
