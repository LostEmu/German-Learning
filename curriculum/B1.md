# Curriculum — B1 (Intermediate)

**CEFR descriptor (B1):** Can understand the main points of clear standard input on familiar matters regularly encountered in work, school, leisure, etc. Can deal with most situations likely to arise while travelling. Can produce simple connected text on topics which are familiar or of personal interest. Can describe experiences, events, dreams, hopes and ambitions and briefly give reasons and explanations for opinions and plans.

**Status:** Not started. *Provisional. Details are set when A2 is complete.*

## Grammar strand

| Topic | Key content |
|---|---|
| Subordinate clauses in depth | weil, dass, ob, wenn, als, obwohl, damit, bevor, nachdem, während, seitdem, bis; verb-final rule; clause-first sentences (*Weil …, gehe ich …*) |
| Indirect questions | *Ich weiß nicht, ob/wann/warum …* |
| Relative clauses | Relative pronouns in nominative, accusative and dative; with prepositions; *was* and *wo* as relatives |
| The genitive case | Articles & noun endings; *wegen, trotz, während, statt*; n-declension nouns |
| Passive voice | Process passive (*werden* + participle) in all main tenses; passive with modal verbs; *von* vs *durch* |
| Konjunktiv II | *würde* + infinitive; wäre, hätte, könnte, müsste; polite requests, hypotheticals, wishes, advice |
| Infinitive constructions | zu + infinitive; um … zu, ohne … zu, statt … zu |
| Past perfect & future | Plusquamperfekt; Futur I (prediction vs intention) |
| Verbs with fixed prepositions | warten auf, denken an, sich interessieren für …; da- and wo-compounds |
| Two-part connectors | sowohl … als auch, entweder … oder, weder … noch, nicht nur … sondern auch |
| Adjective declension consolidation | All three patterns; adjectival nouns (*der/die Studierende, der/die Deutsche*) |

## Skills strand

- **Speaking/writing:** expressing and justifying opinions (*Meiner Meinung nach …*, *Ich bin der Ansicht, dass …*), agreeing/disagreeing, structured paragraphs (topic sentence → support → conclusion), semi-formal and formal emails.
- **Reading:** longer texts (news in simple German, popular science), skimming vs. detailed reading.
- **Listening:** slow news, podcasts, everyday conversations at natural-but-clear speed.
- **First scientific vocabulary:** arithmetic and basic mathematics language (plus, minus, mal, geteilt durch, die Gleichung, die Funktion), units and measurement, describing simple experiments.

## Swiss strand begins (from about mid-B1)

1. Switzerland's linguistic landscape: four national languages; **diglossia** (Swiss German dialects spoken, Standard German written)
2. Swiss Standard German (*Schweizer Hochdeutsch*): no ß, *Helvetismen* (e.g. das Velo, das Trottoir, parkieren, grillieren, der Znüni)
3. Recognising Swiss pronunciation of Standard German
4. Zurich German (*Züritüütsch*) greetings and survival phrases, for **recognition only**

See [vocabulary/swiss-german.md](../vocabulary/swiss-german.md).

## B1 exit criteria

1. Pass a B1 checkpoint modelled on Goethe-Zertifikat B1 (≥ 80%, no section below 70%).
2. Write a 150–200 word argued opinion text with correct subordinate-clause word order.
3. Hold an unscripted conversation on familiar topics (with a human partner, recorded or live).
