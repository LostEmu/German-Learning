# Curriculum — A2 (Elementary)

**CEFR descriptor (A2):** Can understand sentences and frequently used expressions on areas of immediate relevance (personal and family information, shopping, local geography, employment). Can communicate in simple, routine tasks needing a simple and direct exchange of information. Can describe in simple terms aspects of their background and immediate environment.

**Status:** Not started. *This is a provisional plan. Lesson numbers and content will be revised based on A1 results and recorded weaknesses.*

## Practical communication themes (woven through A2)

- Finding accommodation (WG, flat inspection, rental ads, typical of Zurich)
- Public transport & travel (tickets, timetables, platform announcements)
- Health (doctor's appointments, pharmacy, describing symptoms)
- Shopping, banking, post office
- Making and changing appointments; semi-formal emails
- Telling a story about the past (weekend, holidays, life events)
- University life: enrolment, timetables, library, cafeteria

## A2 exit criteria

1. Pass the A2 CEFR checkpoint (≥ 80%, no section below 70%).
2. Every A2 grammar topic at **Secure** or better.
3. Write a 100–120 word text about a past event using the Perfekt correctly.
4. Handle routine transactional dialogues (shop, doctor, transport) without a script.
