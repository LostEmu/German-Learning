# Curriculum — A1 (Absolute Beginner → Beginner)

**CEFR descriptor (A1):** Can understand and use familiar everyday expressions and very basic phrases. Can introduce themselves and others and can ask and answer questions about personal details such as where they live, people they know and things they have. Can interact in a simple way provided the other person talks slowly and clearly and is prepared to help.

**Status:** In progress. Lessons 0–1 started on 2026-11-09.

---

## Design principles

- One major new concept per lesson. Vocabulary is taught in the context of that concept.
- Every lesson ends with an independent assessment (lesson test). Every 5 lessons there is a cumulative assessment; every 10 lessons a major exam.
- Progression is gated: a lesson is "completed" only after its test is marked and the errors are corrected.
- **Curriculum note:** Your outline puts modal verbs and separable verbs at A2. The official A1 exams (e.g. Goethe-Zertifikat A1: *Start Deutsch 1*) already expect *basic* use of both (e.g. *Ich möchte …*, *Ich kann …*, *Ich stehe um 7 Uhr auf*). So they are **introduced** at the end of A1 (Lessons 17–19) and **taught in depth** at A2.

## A1 vocabulary domains

Greetings & courtesy · personal details · countries & languages · family · numbers, time & dates · days, months · food & drink · shopping · home & accommodation · university & studying · daily routine · city & transport (basic).

## A1 exit criteria

To move on to A2 you must:

1. Pass the A1 CEFR checkpoint (≥ 80% overall, no section below 70%).
2. Have every A1 grammar topic at **Secure** or **Mastered**.
3. Be able to write a 60–80 word personal text (e.g. an email introducing yourself) with no more than minor errors.
4. Be able to hold a scripted-to-semi-free introductory conversation in writing (and ideally aloud with a human speaking partner).
