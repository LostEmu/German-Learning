# Curriculum — C1 (Advanced)

**CEFR descriptor (C1):** Can understand a wide range of demanding, longer texts, and recognise implicit meaning. Can express ideas fluently and spontaneously without much obvious searching for expressions. Can use language flexibly and effectively for social, academic and professional purposes. Can produce clear, well-structured, detailed text on complex subjects.

**Status:** Not started. *Long-term ambition, provisional plan.*

---

## Grammar & style strand

- Nominal vs. verbal style: converting between them at will
- Functional verb constructions (*Funktionsverbgefüge*) in depth
- Complex participial constructions, appositions, nested clauses
- Advanced Konjunktiv usage (*als ob*, past irrealis, Konjunktiv in academic hedging)
- Register control: academic, journalistic, administrative, colloquial
- Idioms and fixed expressions (*Redewendungen*)

## Academic & scientific strand

- Reading scientific literature: quantum mechanics, astrophysics & cosmology, computational science
- Following lectures (e.g. recorded ETH Bachelor lectures, which are largely taught in German)
- Seminar skills: asking questions, raising objections, summarising a discussion
- Academic writing: abstract (*Zusammenfassung*), structure of a term paper (*Hausarbeit*), citing and hedging

## Natural spoken German

- Colloquial reductions (*hab ich, isses, haste*), fillers, discourse markers
- Regional variation across Germany, Austria and Switzerland
- Following fast, overlapping conversation

## Swiss strand

- Comfortable comprehension of Zurich German in daily and workplace contexts
- Swiss Standard German in official, academic and administrative writing
- (Optional) active basic Zurich German for social integration
